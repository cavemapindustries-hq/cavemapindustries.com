PRIVACY POLICY

Million Monster Maker · Last updated September 2026

Cave Map Industries LLC (“Cave Map,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) built Million Monster Maker (the “App”) to be a simple, self-contained toy — not a service. This policy explains, plainly, what that means for your privacy.

WE DO NOT COLLECT ANY PERSONAL INFORMATION.

The App:

  • does not require you to create an account or sign in;
  • does not connect to the internet or make any network calls;
  • does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind;
  • does not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information.

WHAT THE APP STORES

The only data the App saves is stored locally on your own device and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • which of the 40 collectible monsters you’ve discovered, so your collection progress is remembered between sessions;
  • your sound on/off preference;
  • whether you’ve already seen the one-time swipe tutorial hint.

None of this is personal information, none of it leaves your device, and none of it is shared with Cave Map or any third party. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

MOTION SENSOR

The App reads your device’s motion sensor while it’s open to tilt your monster’s shadow. These readings are used on the spot and are never saved or sent anywhere.

SHARING YOUR MONSTERS

When you tap Share, the App hands your monster image to iOS’s built-in share sheet. Whatever you choose to share goes directly to the app or person you pick — it never passes through Cave Map.

CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

The App is intended for ages 10 and up. Because it collects no personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect data from children — there is simply no data leaving the device for anyone, of any age.

THIRD PARTIES

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs. Your device’s operating system may collect its own standard diagnostic data under Apple’s privacy policy; that collection is Apple’s, not ours, and is outside this App’s control.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change with it. Since the App makes no network calls, it can’t notify you in-app — check back here if you have questions.

CONTACT US

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@cavemapindustries.com.
